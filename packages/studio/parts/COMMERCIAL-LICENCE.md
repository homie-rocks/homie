# Optional commercial licence starting text

This is a drafting aid, not a selected policy or a substitute for the seller's review.
Copy and adapt it into the release's licenseTerms file, use a LicenseRef identifier,
and hash that file by packing the release. Fill in a complete agreement before sharing.

The seller grants the purchasing studio the rights specified in the signed purchase
schedule for the identified part and release. That schedule's scope, licensed game,
seat count, source inclusion, commercial-use permission, transfer permission, update
coverage and existing-use rights after refund or expiry control over conflicting general
language in this agreement. A seat means one human contributor; authorised build systems
may process the part on that contributor's behalf. A game licence covers only the named
game; a studio licence covers the purchasing studio's team and games.

State the permitted incorporation and distribution in finished games, whether attribution
is required, any restrictions on redistribution of source or standalone assets, support
and delivery availability, and the exact refund procedure and terms. Choose applicable
law, warranties and liability provisions suitable for the seller's circumstances and
mandatory buyer rights. Do not leave these decisions to the assistant. If the structured
schedule cannot express the intended agreement, do not sell through this contract until
both the schema and enforcement have been extended and reviewed.
